Community Connector

Prepare to connect with a person or organization.

Choose the kind of organization, explain what you need help communicating about, and build a respectful message you can edit before sending.

Build your outreach

Only include details you are comfortable sharing. Avoid entering private identification, account, medical, or student-record information.

Your connection plan

Choose the contact, request, and communication topic. An editable message starter will appear before you build the full plan.

What this tool does—and does not do

The Community Connector helps prepare communication. It does not select providers, verify organizations, determine eligibility, make referrals, or replace emergency, legal, medical, financial, or school-system guidance. Confirm official contact information before sharing private information.

This tool is intentionally different from the Goal Planner. The Goal Planner helps define an outcome and steps. The Community Connector helps identify how to ask another person or organization for information, access, collaboration, or support.
