What this tool does—and does not do

The Outreach Message Generator helps prepare communication. It does not select providers, verify organizations, determine eligibility, make referrals, or replace emergency, legal, medical, financial, or school-system guidance. Confirm official contact information before sharing private information.

This tool is intentionally different from the Goal Planner. The Goal Planner helps define an outcome and steps. The Outreach Message Generator helps identify how to ask another person or organization for information, access, collaboration, or support.
