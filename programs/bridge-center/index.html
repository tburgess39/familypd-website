Select one general need. The tool will suggest who may be useful to contact and help you prepare for that conversation.

Your connection plan will appear here.

It will include people or organizations to consider, questions to ask, a sample outreach message, and follow-up steps.

How this differs from the Goal Builder

The Goal Builder helps a family choose an outcome and plan steps. The Community Connector helps a family identify who could support them, prepare outreach, and strengthen relationships with people and organizations.

A connection is more than a link

A useful connection includes a real contact, clear expectations, respectful communication, and a follow-up step. When possible, a warm introduction from someone trusted is stronger than simply handing a family a phone number.

Do not wait for a crisis

Families—especially single parents and isolated caregivers—benefit from building support before an emergency. A dependable network can help with information, transportation, child care, mentoring, emotional support, and opportunities.

Educational use and safety

This tool provides general preparation ideas. It does not make referrals, verify organizations, determine eligibility, provide professional advice, or replace emergency services. Protect private information and confirm an organization’s identity, cost, privacy practices, and services before sharing personal details.

FamilyPD places community among its four guiding principles because families benefit from trusted people, shared information, collaboration, and support beyond the household.
