9/1/26 Advancement Reminders

Always verify current annual dates and announcements on the official PGS page.

October 1

The 9/1/26 Reference Guide states that all activities completed during the advancement cycle must be submitted by October 1 of the year the employee is advancing.

Current window only

Activities completed during a previous advancement window will not be accepted for the current submission window.

Verify other dates

Check the official PGS page for current advancement-request, completion, and announcement dates not stated in the 9/1/26 Reference Guide table.

Which advancement track applies?

The 9/1/26 Reference Guide identifies a two-year track for educators/licensed professionals serving exclusively at a Title I school and for self-contained special education teachers. Non-Title I educators and centrally assigned employees generally use the three-year track; centrally assigned employees may qualify for the two-year track only when all assignments are in Title I schools.
