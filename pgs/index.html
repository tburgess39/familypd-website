Effective September 1, 2026

What changed in the new PGS Reference Guide?

The FamilyPD assistant uses these changes only when the activity date is September 1, 2026 or later. Final eligibility and approval remain with CCSD.

2/3

Advancement tracks

Educators/licensed professionals serving exclusively at a Title I school and self-contained special education teachers are eligible for the two-year track. Non-Title I educators use the three-year track. Centrally assigned employees generally use the three-year track unless all assignments are in Title I schools.

80/40

Conference attendance split

In-person/live professional-development conference attendance has an 80-CU maximum. Virtual/synchronous conference attendance has a 40-CU maximum. The new guide does not list a standalone asynchronous conference/webinar attendance category.

50

Micro-credentials

Only Digital Promise-approved micro-credentials may be used, with a 50-CU maximum. The 9/1/26 reference-guide table does not state a universal CU value per credential, so FamilyPD no longer assumes 5 CUs each for new-era activities.

120

NDE professional development

Nevada Department of Education professional development is listed with a 120-CU maximum. The category is limited to free activities provided by NDE staff and excludes activities educators/licensed professionals must pay to attend.

Understand the system before using the assistant

CCEA, Contact Units, and salary advancement

The Professional Growth System was developed through the CCSD–CCEA negotiated process. Contact Units, or CUs, are the units licensed educators accrue toward movement across the Professional Salary Table. The assistant helps organize information, but CCSD and CCEA guidance remain the official authorities.

CU

What is a Contact Unit?

Under current guidance, one unpaid CU generally represents three qualifying hours. A paid supplemental-rate or stipend activity generally earns one CU per six qualifying hours unless a listed exception applies. Category rules, limits, and documentation still matter.

225

Column advancement

Successful accrual of 225 approved CUs is used for one column movement. The time available to complete that movement depends on the employee's eligible two-year or three-year track, and current annual requirements must still be verified on the official CCSD PGS page.

CCEA

CCEA member support

CCEA provides a PGS Companion Guide, virtual support, professional-learning opportunities, microcredentials, and assistance for members who believe CUs were wrongfully denied.

ELMS

Where official records live

ELMS is used for CCSD professional-learning registration and for submitting and tracking PGS documentation. FamilyPD does not submit, approve, or store official CU records.

A missing form, unclear category, documentation problem, or denial may have a solution. Getting timely help can protect the work you already completed and help you avoid leaving possible salary advancement on the table.
