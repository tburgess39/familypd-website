Why These Clues Matter

The risk grows when separate facts are combined.
Connect Clues

Addresses, schedules, geotags, property photos, and live vacation posts can create stalking, burglary, or unwanted-contact risk.

Names and relationships help attackers impersonate trusted people and write convincing emergency stories.

Birth dates, hobbies, teams, and pet names can support password guessing, security-question attacks, and targeted messages.

Home values, purchases, occupations, and lifestyle clues help scammers estimate wealth and personalize fraud.
