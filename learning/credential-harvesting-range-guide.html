Verify the request

A message claims your school account will close today. The sign-in screen looks familiar, but the destination differs from the school’s published sign-in address. Close the page, open the known school portal independently, and ask the support desk to verify the request. Report the message through the school’s approved process.

Respond after possible exposure

If someone entered a password, contact the support desk through a trusted channel immediately. Use the approved process to reset the password, revoke affected sessions, review sign-in activity, and secure recovery methods. Preserve the suspicious message as instructed. Keep passwords and verification codes out of support tickets.
