Pillar One

Health

Protect the foundation: physical, mental, emotional, spiritual health, rest, and safety.

LearnExploreDiscussApplyGo Further
Why this pillar matters

Health comes first because every other pillar becomes harder to sustain when the body, mind, or environment is unsafe or overwhelmed.

Learn

Build understanding before trying to fix everything.

Health is more than the absence of illness. It includes your body, thoughts, emotions, sense of meaning, and safety.

Small systems—appointments, sleep routines, check-ins, and emergency information—make healthy choices easier to repeat.

FamilyPD approach: Start small, look for patterns, and build systems that make the healthier choice easier to repeat.

Discuss

Talk about it together.

  • What helps each person in our household feel safe and supported?
  • Which health habit would make the biggest difference this month?

Family meeting tip

Choose one prompt. Give everyone a chance to answer without interruption. End by identifying one shared takeaway—not by forcing immediate agreement.

Apply

Turn the lesson into one small action.

  1. Choose one health habit to track for seven days.
  2. Schedule one overdue health, dental, or wellness task.
  3. Create or review your household emergency information.
