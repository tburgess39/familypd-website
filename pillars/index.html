The FamilyPD framework

Five pillars that work together.

Health, relationships, education and skills, finances, and goals form a practical sequence for building a stronger household. Families may work across pillars, but the order protects the foundation.

Four guiding principles

Use the same building tools across every pillar.

Self-awareness

Notice patterns, strengths, needs, beliefs, triggers, and barriers before choosing a response.

Systems

Create repeatable ways to handle work, communication, learning, money, and progress.

Policies

Agree on expectations, boundaries, responsibilities, and what happens when plans are not followed.

Community

Use family, schools, professionals, mentors, organizations, and credible information instead of carrying everything alone.
