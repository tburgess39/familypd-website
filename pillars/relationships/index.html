Pillar Two

Relationships

Build relationships that support growth instead of slowly draining it.

LearnExploreDiscussApplyGo Further
Why this pillar matters

Healthy relationships create trust, belonging, accountability, and support. They also make it easier to learn, recover, and pursue goals together.

Learn

Build understanding before trying to fix everything.

Strong relationships do not avoid every disagreement. They create safer ways to communicate, set boundaries, repair harm, and stay connected.

Understanding differences—including communication styles—can reduce blame and help family members work together.

FamilyPD approach: Start small, look for patterns, and build systems that make the healthier choice easier to repeat.

Discuss

Talk about it together.

  • What makes you feel heard and respected?
  • What boundary or expectation needs to be clearer in our home?

Family meeting tip

Choose one prompt. Give everyone a chance to answer without interruption. End by identifying one shared takeaway—not by forcing immediate agreement.

Apply

Turn the lesson into one small action.

  1. Hold a 15-minute family check-in.
  2. Practice one listening skill without interrupting.
  3. Complete the Communication Style Reflection and compare takeaways.
