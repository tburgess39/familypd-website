Pillar Five

Goals & Purpose

Give your family’s effort a clear direction without ignoring the foundation underneath it.

LearnExploreDiscussApplyGo Further
Why this pillar matters

Goals help turn values and hopes into action. They work best when health, relationships, learning, and finances are considered together.

Learn

Build understanding before trying to fix everything.

Motivation changes. Systems, habits, accountability, and small next steps help people continue when motivation is low.

Procrastination is often a signal—not simply laziness. A task may feel unclear, overwhelming, boring, risky, or disconnected from what matters.

FamilyPD approach: Start small, look for patterns, and build systems that make the healthier choice easier to repeat.

Discuss

Talk about it together.

  • What are we working toward as a family?
  • What is one “procrastination monster” blocking progress right now?

Family meeting tip

Choose one prompt. Give everyone a chance to answer without interruption. End by identifying one shared takeaway—not by forcing immediate agreement.

Apply

Turn the lesson into one small action.

  1. Break one delayed task into a step that takes 10 minutes or less.
  2. Choose one weekly family goal and assign a clear owner.
  3. Review progress at the next family meeting and adjust the system—not just the person.
