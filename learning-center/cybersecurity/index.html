Core security idea

Protect confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

Confidentiality

Only authorized people should be able to access information.

Family example: A school portal requires the correct account and authentication.

Integrity

Information should remain accurate and protected from unauthorized change.

Family example: A payment amount or school record should not be secretly altered.

Availability

Authorized people should be able to reach systems and information when needed.

Family example: Backups and emergency information remain accessible after a device failure.

Family practice

Strengthen one part of your family’s digital safety system.

Review access

Choose an important family account. Identify who can sign in, which devices are connected, and whether old users or devices should be removed.

Improve recovery

Check multifactor authentication, recovery information, backups, and who the family should contact when something suspicious happens.
