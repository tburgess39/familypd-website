Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting devices, accounts, networks, and information from harm, misuse, disruption, or unauthorized access.

Why this matters

Families use connected technology every day. Basic cybersecurity habits protect privacy, money, schoolwork, relationships, and future opportunities.

Key vocabulary

Confidentiality

Keeping information available only to authorized people.

Integrity

Keeping information accurate and protected from unauthorized changes.

Availability

Making sure systems and information are accessible when needed.

Phishing

A deceptive message designed to make someone reveal information, send money, or open something unsafe.

Learn → Explore → Discuss → Apply

Learn

Understand that strong security combines people, processes, and technology.

Explore

Review passwords, updates, backups, privacy settings, and common scams.

Discuss

Ask what information your family considers private and how it should be shared.

Apply

Turn on multifactor authentication, update devices, and practice checking suspicious messages before clicking.

Try this together

Choose one family account. Confirm that the password is unique, multifactor authentication is enabled, recovery information is current, and everyone knows how to recognize an unexpected sign-in alert.
