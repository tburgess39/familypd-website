22.1 • Change and asset management

Document purpose, scope, systems, dependencies, risk, affected users, approval, window, owner, verification, and rollback. A change advisory board may review risk and timing. Record acceptance from the owner after functional verification. Inventory tracks asset identifier, owner, location, warranty, licensing, and disposal status. Record changes as they happen so support can trust the inventory.

22.2 • Electrical, environmental, and ESD safety

Disconnect equipment as instructed before servicing and follow workplace electrical procedures. Never open a power supply; refer it to authorized service. Use approved grounded ESD protection for components and keep cables out of walkways. A UPS supplies temporary power during outages; a surge protector limits transient spikes. Follow the safety data sheet and local handling procedures for batteries, toner, and other materials.

22.3 • Scripting basics and validation

A script can read inventory, check disk-space thresholds, or gather status consistently. Identify the supported runtime and extension, such as PowerShell .ps1, batch .bat, shell .sh, Python .py, or JavaScript .js. Variables hold data, conditionals choose an action, and loops repeat checks. Validate inputs, review downloaded code before use, test read-only behavior on a small sample, log failures, and avoid embedding credentials.

Predict a script report

With a threshold of less than 20 GB, inputs of 8 GB, 20 GB, 45 GB, no response, and invalid data should produce low, within threshold, within threshold, unreachable, and invalid reading. Use these fixtures before a pilot. The 20 GB row catches a boundary error and the last two rows verify that missing values cannot be mistaken for measured disk space.
