Socket

CPU SITS in a socket.

Slot

RAM/cards SLIDE into slots.

Port

A portal to the outside: USB, HDMI, Ethernet.

Header

Hidden inside: front panel, internal USB/audio, fan headers.

Connector

Broad term for a physical electrical connection.

Interface

The connection/communication system: SATA, PCIe, USB.

A+ goal: identify CPU socket, DIMM slots, M.2, SATA, PCIe, 24-pin power, CPU power, fan/front-panel headers, rear I/O, and form-factor/compatibility relationships before Mission 04 assembly.
