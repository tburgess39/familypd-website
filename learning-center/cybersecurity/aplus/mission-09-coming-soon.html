Mission goal

Set up mobile devices and peripherals, configure mobile apps, install laptop hardware, and troubleshoot mobile-device problems.

9.1 • Mobile devices & peripherals

  • Digitizer/touch and gesture support
  • Trackpad/touchpad and stylus
  • Microphone, camera/webcam, speakers, headset
  • USB-C and other wired connectors
  • HDMI, VGA, Thunderbolt where supported
  • Port replicators and docking stations
Technician habit: identify the exact port and device model before choosing an adapter or cable.

Mobile connectivity

Mobile devices may use Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, NFC, cellular data, tethering/hotspots, and location services. A symptom can come from the radio, configuration, permissions, account, app, or physical hardware.

Scenario thinking: first determine whether the problem affects one app, one radio, one network, or the entire device.

Apps, accounts & synchronization

Mobile support often means configuring accounts and deciding what data should synchronize. Confirm the user account, network access, permissions, sync settings, and whether the service itself is available.

ClueWhat to inspect
Mail not syncingAccount credentials, server settings, network, sync permissions.
App cannot use camera/micOperating-system privacy permissions.
Location feature failsLocation/GPS setting, app permission, signal/environment.
Bluetooth accessory failsPairing state, battery, range, existing paired devices.

Laptop hardware

Laptops use compact, model-specific assemblies. Before replacement, check service documentation, power down safely, disconnect power where possible, use ESD precautions, and track screws/connectors.

Common service areas include storage, memory, battery, keyboard/trackpad, display assemblies, wireless cards, and cooling components, depending on model.

9.4 • Troubleshoot mobile devices

  • Power/battery: charging, battery health, adapter, heat.
  • Hardware: overheating, liquid/physical damage, dust/moisture.
  • Screen: backlight, broken glass, digitizer, calibration.
  • Connectivity: interference, antenna connections, network tests.
  • Malware: unexpected data use, background processes, changed permissions, blocked downloads.
Check: Touch image displays correctly, but taps do not register. Which component is a strong suspect?

The digitizer/touch-input layer.
