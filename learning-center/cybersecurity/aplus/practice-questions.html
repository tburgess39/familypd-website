CompTIA A+ • Practice Lab

Practice the decision—not just the definition.

These FamilyPD questions are original study questions aligned to A+ concepts. Use them to practice reading clues, eliminating tempting answers, and choosing the BEST or NEXT technician action.

How to read an A+ scenario

FIRST / NEXT asks for sequence. BEST / MOST appropriate asks you to choose among answers that may all sound possible. MOST likely asks you to use the clues to identify the strongest cause. Before answering, identify the user problem, the scope, recent changes, safety/authorization limits, and what evidence is already provided.

Original FamilyPD practice

Unit 1: IT Specialist & troubleshooting mindset

Choose an answer before opening the rationale. The explanation is the most important part.

ScenarioNEXT action

1. A user says, “My monitor stopped working.” The desktop power light is on. What should the technician do NEXT?

Best answer: B. The symptom does not yet prove a failed monitor or computer component. A technician should gather information and check simple physical causes before performing expensive or destructive changes. Question-writing clue: “NEXT” rewards proper sequence.
ScopeTroubleshooting

2. One employee cannot reach an internal website, but coworkers nearby can. Which action is MOST appropriate first?

Best answer: C. The scope clue is that other users are working normally. Start with the affected client instead of changing infrastructure that is serving everyone else. Exam habit: always identify whether the problem affects one device, a group, or the entire environment.
Recent changeTheory

3. A computer became slow immediately after a new application was installed. Which is the BEST initial theory to investigate?

Best answer: A. Recent changes are valuable evidence. Check the change most closely associated with the symptom, then use resource data to support or reject the theory.
AuthorizationEscalation

4. A technician identifies a failed component, but opening the device would violate the vendor warranty and company policy. What should the technician do?

Best answer: D. Technical ability does not override authorization, policy, warranty, or scope. A professional technician knows when escalation is the correct solution.
VerificationBEST answer

5. A technician applies a fix and the reported symptom disappears. What should happen NEXT?

Best answer: B. “The symptom disappeared” is not the same as “the system has been verified.” Check functionality, apply appropriate prevention if relevant, and leave useful documentation.
Performance-based thinkingOrdering

6. Put the following technician actions in the BEST order: (1) verify functionality, (2) identify the problem, (3) test the theory, (4) document, (5) establish a theory, (6) plan and implement the solution.

Best answer: C. Identify → establish a theory → test → plan/implement → verify → document. Even though CompTIA states the methodology itself is not a formal scored 220-1201 objective, this process develops the reasoning students need for troubleshooting scenarios.

Union Test Prep

Do not train yourself to memorize question banks.

After every missed question, record the objective/concept, the clue you missed, why the correct answer is better, and one new example. If you only memorize the wording of a question, a different scenario can still defeat you.
