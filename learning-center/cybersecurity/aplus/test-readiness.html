How to rate yourself

Check the skill only if you could show it—not just recognize the words.

Examples are practice prompts, not a complete list of everything that may appear on the exam.

IDENTIFYI can recognize it.

I can name the part, term, port, symptom, tool, or setting when I see it.

EXPLAINI understand why/how.

I can explain the purpose, difference, benefit, risk, or relationship in my own words.

USE / CONFIGUREI can do it.

I could perform the task, choose the right tool/setting, or describe the steps without a step-by-step guide.

TROUBLESHOOTI can solve a problem.

I can use symptoms and evidence to identify a likely cause, test it, and choose an appropriate fix.

Good self-check: If a classmate asked “How do you know?” or “Show me,” could you explain or demonstrate it? If not yet, leave the box unchecked and use it as a study target.
