Supporting Mobile Software

20.1 • Mobile data and device management

Require a compliant lock, device encryption, and approved MFA. MDM manages device policy and inventory; app management can separate work data from personal data. For a lost phone, verify ownership, policy, and device identity. Lock or remove corporate data where supported and approved. Full-device wipe is destructive and may be inappropriate for personal devices. Connectivity affects when a remote action completes.

20.2 • App and network diagnosis

Identify scope by testing one app versus several, and Wi-Fi versus cellular. Check status, free storage, permissions, data limits, app version, and OS compatibility. Reproduce with a safe account or test file, compare a known-good device, and protect local data before clearing app storage or reinstalling. On a managed phone inspect compliance, VPN, and work-profile configuration before removing profiles.

20.3 • Mobile security symptoms

Unexpected app installs, profile changes, recurring redirects, or rapid battery drain warrant investigation. Rooting or jailbreaking can weaken normal platform protections. Verify app source and publisher, review device profiles and permissions, and use approved mobile security and support steps. Preserve evidence and avoid entering credentials on a potentially compromised device.

Isolate a mobile failure

The mail app fails only on Wi-Fi. Its account works on cellular and the browser can open other websites. Compare the approved Wi-Fi and VPN path with another managed device. Record whether the work mail service alone is affected. Preserve the enrolled profile until you know whether the policy or network path caused the failure.

Practice and readiness check

Choose the best answer for each question. Explain which clue supports your choice. The final question continues the technician case.

A departing employee’s personal phone contains managed work mail. Which action best fits if policy permits?
Explanation

Selective removal limits the action to the managed data.

Only a meeting app’s camera fails; the native camera works. What should you check first?
Explanation

The camera hardware works. The failure is scoped to one app.

A managed phone suddenly reports an unknown configuration profile. What is the best next step?
Explanation

An unknown profile can change trust or network behavior and deserves controlled investigation.

The console shows a remote action as pending. How should the ticket describe it?
Explanation

The management queue confirms the request, while completion needs device acknowledgement or equivalent evidence.
