Concept: laser uses toner (powder) and a fuser (heat).

Other printer types

Inkjet: sprays liquid ink through a print head/nozzles; maintain cartridges/tanks, rollers, alignment and print-head cleaning.

Thermal: heat-based printing; common for receipts/shipping/barcode labels; paper debris can require cleaning.

Impact/dot matrix: print head strikes a ribbon; useful with multipart forms and tractor-fed paper.

10.3 • Troubleshoot by symptom

ProblemFirst checks / likely direction
Offline/no printPower/online state, consumables, test page, cable/Wi-Fi, firmware/driver.
Paper jam / feedClear paper path correctly, paper settings, rollers, seated cartridges/print head.
Faded/faintPrint resolution/quality setting and toner/ink level.
Smudged tonerFuser problem is a key laser-printer clue.
Lines/stripesRollers, charge roller/high-voltage components depending on pattern/printer.
Missing colorCartridge/ink and contacts; test after correction.
Frozen queueClear obsolete jobs; restart print spooler and clear cache as appropriate.
Garbled outputVerify correct PDL/driver; test page helps isolate application vs printer.
Troubleshooting order: power → paper → consumables → connection → queue → driver/settings → hardware maintenance.
Check: A laser page comes out with toner that wipes/smudges off. What component should you suspect?

The fuser, because it uses heat to bond toner to the paper.
