PGS at a glance

The basics without the oversized blocks.

225

Contact Units

Column movement occurs after successful accrual of 225 approved CUs and completion of the required advancement process.

3 / 6

Hours per CU

One CU generally equals 3 eligible unpaid hours or 6 paid supplemental-rate/stipend hours, unless a category states otherwise.

2 / 3

Advancement tracks

Title I-only educators and self-contained special education teachers may use the two-year track; other eligible employees generally use the three-year track.

June 1

Request deadline

Submit the Request for Column Advancement for the next contract year by June 1.

Aug. 31

Completion deadline

Complete all hours and coursework being used for the current advancement cycle by August 31.

Oct. 1

ELMS deadline

Submit required documentation in ELMS by October 1 of the year the employee is advancing.

September 1, 2026 update

Important changes to know.

  • Conferences: in-person/live attendance remains capped at 80 CUs; virtual/synchronous attendance is capped at 40 CUs.
  • Asynchronous conferences: the 9/1/26 reference-guide table does not list a standalone asynchronous conference/webinar attendance category.
  • Digital Promise micro-credentials: the new guide lists a 50-CU maximum but does not state one universal CU value per credential in the table.
  • Nevada Department of Education PD: qualifying free NDE professional development is listed with a 120-CU maximum.
  • Paid exceptions: qualifying Title I PLC, tutoring, and mentoring activities outside the contractual day may receive full CUs where the guide specifically permits it.
  • Single-file evidence: self-reported documentation is prepared as one file in CCSD Google Drive and the individual file link is submitted through ELMS.

CCSD & CCEA announcement notices

Dated rules that can affect otherwise eligible activities.

These notices are tracked in the FamilyPD PGS Assistant because the activity date can change the result. Always verify the live joint announcement document before relying on a notice.

Official reference: CCSD currently links the joint CCSD and CCEA PGS Announcements Impacting Contact Unit Accrual ↗ from its PGS page.
Effective Feb. 15, 2024

LinkedIn Learning

Qualifying post-date LinkedIn Learning CU accrual is limited to the CTE process for CTE teachers; non-CTE licensed employees cannot use post-date LinkedIn Learning activities under the tracked announcement.

Contact: cteinccsd@nv.ccsd.net

Effective Sept. 13, 2022

Self-designed graduate course transcripts

Courses that convert self-identified professional-development hours into university credit are not accepted for CU accrual when completed on or after this date.

Contact: pgs@nv.ccsd.net

Effective May 27, 2021

Idaho State University COVID-19 transcripts

Credits earned after this date through the identified ISU COVID-19 professional-learning-to-credit process are not accepted for CU accrual.

Contact: pgs@nv.ccsd.net

Effective Apr. 14, 2023

Southern Utah University EDPD 5000–5999

Specified SUU credits issued from CCSD professional-learning certificates without additional SUU instruction are not accepted for additional CU accrual after this date.

Contact: pgs@nv.ccsd.net

Effective Mar. 12, 2026

Mental Health Academy coursework for counselors

Coursework must align with the counselor assignment or licensure. Only identified approved courses may accrue CUs, and a Time-Based Activities form may be required.

Contact: Guidance and Counseling Department, 702-799-8441

Source-check reminder

Microcredential information may differ by page

CCEA's public microcredential page describes 5-CU credentials and up to 100 CUs, while the 9/1/26 PGS Reference Guide lists Digital Promise micro-credentials with a 50-CU maximum. For activities on or after September 1, 2026, verify the current PGS rule before relying on a promotional page.

FamilyPD scope: the five dated notices above mirror the announcement rules currently encoded in the PGS Assistant; the live CCSD/CCEA announcement document remains the controlling source for updates.
