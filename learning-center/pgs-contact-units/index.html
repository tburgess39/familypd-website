CCEA, Contact Units, and the Professional Growth System

A plain-language starting point for CCSD licensed educators who are organizing professional learning for salary advancement.

Open the FamilyPD PGS Guide Open My PGS Assistant

What is PGS?

The CCSD Professional Growth System allows licensed educators to accrue Contact Units toward movement across the Professional Salary Table. Column movement follows successful accrual of 225 approved CUs, together with the required annual request and submission process (Clark County School District [CCSD], 2026).

What is a CU?

A Contact Unit is a unit used within PGS. Current guidance generally treats three qualifying unpaid hours as one CU and six qualifying paid supplemental-rate or stipend hours as one CU, unless the activity has a fixed value or an exception (Clark County Education Association [CCEA], 2026).

How CCEA helps

CCEA offers a PGS Companion Guide, member support sessions, professional-learning partners, microcredentials, and assistance when a member believes CUs or salary advancement were wrongfully denied (CCEA, 2026).

How FamilyPD helps

The FamilyPD PGS Assistant helps an educator identify a likely activity category, estimate possible CUs, organize evidence, and prepare for ELMS. It does not approve CUs, replace official forms, submit records, or determine eligibility.

Do not let confusion keep you from pursuing salary advancement.

PGS can feel overwhelming when you are balancing students, family, professional learning, evidence, ELMS, and annual deadlines. Before becoming frustrated or giving up, ask for help. A missing document, unclear category, denial, or missed step may have a solution.

Your professional growth may support your financial growth. Staying organized and requesting help early can protect work you already completed and reduce the chance of leaving possible salary advancement on the table.
