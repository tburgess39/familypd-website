CCEA, Contact Units, and the Professional Growth System

A plain-language starting point for CCSD licensed educators who are organizing professional learning for salary advancement.

PGS rule update effective September 1, 2026

FamilyPD now distinguishes between two supported rule eras. Activities from May 1, 2024 through August 31, 2026 remain under the prior rule set. Activities on or after September 1, 2026 use the PGS Reference Guide effective September 1, 2026. The activity date matters when using the assistant.

Important changes: virtual/synchronous conference attendance is capped at 40 CUs; in-person/live conference attendance remains capped at 80 CUs; the new guide does not list a standalone asynchronous conference/webinar attendance category; Digital Promise micro-credentials have a 50-CU maximum but no universal per-credential CU value stated in the reference-guide table; and qualifying free Nevada Department of Education professional development is listed with a 120-CU maximum.

What is PGS?

The CCSD Professional Growth System allows educators/licensed professionals to accrue Contact Units toward movement across the Professional Salary Table. Column movement occurs after successful accrual of 225 approved CUs (Clark County School District [CCSD], 2026).

What is a CU?

A Contact Unit is a unit used within PGS. Under the 9/1/26 guide, one CU generally equals 180 minutes (3 hours). If the educator/licensed professional is paid the supplemental instruction rate or a stipend for the professional-development activity, one CU generally equals 360 minutes (6 hours), unless the guide states an exception (CCSD, 2026).

How CCEA helps

CCEA offers a PGS Companion Guide, member support sessions, professional-learning partners, microcredentials, and assistance when a member believes CUs or salary advancement were wrongfully denied (CCEA, 2026).

How FamilyPD helps

The FamilyPD PGS Assistant helps an educator identify a likely activity category, estimate possible CUs, organize evidence, and prepare for ELMS. It does not approve CUs, replace official forms, submit records, or determine eligibility.

Which advancement track?

Educators/licensed professionals serving exclusively at a Title I school and self-contained special education teachers are eligible for the two-year track. Non-Title I educators are eligible for the three-year track. Centrally assigned employees are generally eligible only for the three-year track unless all assignments are in Title I schools (CCSD, 2026).

What must be submitted?

For self-reported activities, the required documentation is uploaded to the educator/licensed professional's CCSD Google Drive as a single file, shared so anyone in the Clark County School District with the link can view, and then submitted through Add Advancement Activity in ELMS. The submission deadline for activities completed during the advancement cycle is October 1 of the year the employee is advancing (CCSD, 2026).

Do not let confusion keep you from pursuing salary advancement.

PGS can feel overwhelming when you are balancing students, family, professional learning, evidence, ELMS, and annual deadlines. Before becoming frustrated or giving up, ask for help. A missing document, unclear category, denial, or missed step may have a solution.

Your professional growth may support your financial growth. Staying organized and requesting help early can protect work you already completed and reduce the chance of leaving possible salary advancement on the table.
