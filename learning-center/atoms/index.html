Atoms

Atoms are the tiny building blocks that make up ordinary matter. Everything from water and air to a desk or a person is made of atoms.

Why this matters

Understanding atoms helps learners make sense of chemistry, materials, electricity, biology, and many everyday changes in the world around them.

Key vocabulary

Atom

The smallest unit of an element that still has that element’s properties.

Proton

A positively charged particle in the nucleus.

Neutron

A particle with no electric charge in the nucleus.

Electron

A negatively charged particle found around the nucleus.

Learn → Explore → Discuss → Apply

Learn

An atom has a central nucleus containing protons and neutrons, with electrons around it.

Explore

The number of protons identifies the element. Carbon always has six protons.

Discuss

Ask why two objects can look different even though both are made of atoms.

Apply

Build a simple model using small household objects to represent protons, neutrons, and electrons.

Try this together

Use two colors of small objects for protons and neutrons and a third color for electrons. Build a carbon atom with six protons, six neutrons, and six electrons. Label each part and explain which number determines the element.
