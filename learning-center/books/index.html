Books that shaped our thinking

Books That Shaped FamilyPD

Explore the books that influenced FamilyPD ideas about growth, healing, leadership, relationships, finances, education, and purpose.

Mindset & Goals

Think and Grow Rich

Author: Napoleon Hill

What we explored: Definite purpose, persistence, belief, planning, and action.

FamilyPD lens: Connect goals to consistent household systems—not wishful thinking alone.

Mindset & Goals

Outwitting the Devil

Author: Napoleon Hill

What we explored: Fear, drifting, habits, self-direction, and disciplined thinking.

FamilyPD lens: Notice patterns that keep a family stuck and build routines that support intentional choices.

Leadership & Systems

Built to Last

Author: Jim Collins and Jerry I. Porras

What we explored: Core values, enduring purpose, and organizations built to last.

FamilyPD lens: Clarify family mission, values, and traditions that can carry across generations.

Leadership & Systems

Good to Great

Author: Jim Collins

What we explored: Focus, disciplined people, disciplined thought, and consistent action.

FamilyPD lens: Use a few clear priorities, shared roles, and regular review instead of constant reaction.

Leadership & Courage

Dare to Lead

Author: Brené Brown

What we explored: Courage, vulnerability, trust, and difficult conversations.

FamilyPD lens: Create a home where people can speak honestly, repair harm, and learn from mistakes.

Leadership & Contribution

Linchpin

Author: Seth Godin

What we explored: Initiative, creativity, contribution, and becoming indispensable through value.

FamilyPD lens: Help each family member recognize strengths and make meaningful contributions.

Relationships

Boundaries

Author: Henry Cloud and John Townsend

What we explored: Limits, responsibility, consequences, and healthy connection.

FamilyPD lens: Clarify what each person owns and how the family responds to harmful behavior.

Healing & Growth

Changes That Heal

Author: Henry Cloud

What we explored: Bonding, boundaries, accepting good and bad, and growing into adulthood.

FamilyPD lens: Support reflection, emotional maturity, and healthier patterns across generations.

Identity & Resilience

Finding Me

Author: Viola Davis

What we explored: Identity, childhood adversity, courage, healing, and self-acceptance.

FamilyPD lens: Honor family stories while deciding what to heal, change, and carry forward.

Identity & Healing

I’m Glad My Mom Died

Author: Jennette McCurdy

What we explored: Family roles, control, trauma, boundaries, and recovery.

FamilyPD lens: Recognize how childhood dynamics can shape adulthood and why honest repair matters.

Life Story & Perspective

Greenlights

Author: Matthew McConaughey

What we explored: Reflection, risk, resilience, choices, and learning from life.

FamilyPD lens: Use family stories and turning points as lessons instead of hiding them.

Presence & Well-Being

The Power of Now

Author: Eckhart Tolle

What we explored: Presence, awareness, thought patterns, and inner peace.

FamilyPD lens: Slow down, notice reactions, and make room for calm before responding.

Purpose & Journey

The Alchemist

Author: Paulo Coelho

What we explored: Purpose, courage, setbacks, learning, and the meaning found in the journey.

FamilyPD lens: Help families pursue goals while paying attention to growth along the way.

Perspective

How to Hold a Cockroach

Author: Matthew Maxwell

What we explored: Interpretation, discomfort, judgment, and changing how we relate to problems.

FamilyPD lens: Pause and examine the story attached to a difficult situation before reacting.

Human Behavior

The Laws of Human Nature

Author: Robert Greene

What we explored: Emotions, patterns, motives, self-awareness, and relationships.

FamilyPD lens: Study behavior with curiosity so families can communicate and respond more intentionally.

Relationships & Letting Go

The Let Them Theory

Author: Mel Robbins

What we explored: Releasing control of others and redirecting energy toward personal choices.

FamilyPD lens: Allow people to show who they are, then choose boundaries and actions that protect well-being.

Finances

The Total Money Makeover

Author: Dave Ramsey

What we explored: Budgeting, emergency savings, debt reduction, and financial habits.

FamilyPD lens: Compare strategies, build a plan, and choose methods that fit the family’s real circumstances.

About these book pages

FamilyPD credits the original authors and encourages readers to access the original books. Summaries reflect what FamilyPD learned and how we interpreted or applied selected ideas. They do not reproduce the books and are not endorsements by the authors or publishers.
