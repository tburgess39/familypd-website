Books that shaped our thinking

What We Read—and What We Learned

These are not replacements for the books or formal reviews. They are FamilyPD learning summaries that identify the original author, explain what stood out, and connect ideas to family growth.

Finances

The Total Money Makeover

Author: Dave Ramsey

What we explored: Emergency savings, budgeting, and the debt snowball approach.

FamilyPD lens: Quick wins can build momentum, while families should also compare approaches and choose what fits their needs.

Mindset & Goals

Think and Grow Rich

Author: Napoleon Hill

What we explored: Definite purpose, repeated thought, persistence, and action.

FamilyPD lens: Mindset matters, while systems, access, relationships, and real-world barriers matter too.

Full learning page planned

Relationships

Boundaries

Authors: Henry Cloud and John Townsend

What we explored: Responsibility, limits, consequences, and healthy relationships.

FamilyPD lens: Boundaries are not about controlling others; they clarify what we will accept and how we will respond.

Full learning page planned

Leadership

Good to Great

Author: Jim Collins

What we explored: Focus, disciplined people, disciplined thought, and consistent systems.

FamilyPD lens: Organizational practices can be adapted carefully to create clarity and shared responsibility at home.

FamilyPD credits the original authors and encourages readers to access the original books. Summaries reflect what FamilyPD learned and how we interpreted or applied selected ideas. They do not reproduce the books and are not endorsements by the authors or publishers.
