FamilyPD overview

Use the district site as the source of truth.

CCSD families use a combination of the district website, the student’s school website, Infinite Campus, and official department pages. Begin with the school for student-specific questions and use district pages to verify policies, programs, and systemwide requirements.

District pages, tools, deadlines, and policies can change. Use this guide to find the right official location, then confirm the information that applies to the student.

Monitor progress

Use Infinite Campus as the regular family dashboard

What families can see

CCSD identifies Infinite Campus as its student information system for grades, assignments, attendance, schedules, academic plans, reports, fees, and district notices (Clark County School District [CCSD], n.d.-a).

A practical routine

Choose one weekly check-in day. Look for missing evidence, grade changes, attendance errors, upcoming assessments, and messages. Ask the student what the information means before deciding on a response.

Understand grades

Separate practice, mastery, and habits of learning

Formative and summative work

CCSD describes formative assessment as feedback used during learning and summative assessment as evidence of learning after instruction. District grading guidance generally weights summative evidence more heavily (CCSD, n.d.-b).

Ask the teacher

When a grade is confusing, ask which assignments are formative or summative, whether evidence is missing, what reassessment is available, and how non-academic habits are reported.

Plan for graduation

Review the academic plan before senior year

Credits and diploma pathways

CCSD directs students to participate in academic planning with a counselor, enroll in coursework aligned with goals, and have parents or guardians review the plan. Diploma and endorsement requirements vary, so review the current official page and the student’s transcript (CCSD, n.d.-c).

Family questions

  • How many credits are earned and still needed?
  • Which required courses remain?
  • Is the student on track for the intended diploma or endorsement?
  • How do CTE, dual credit, AP, IB, or work-based learning fit the plan?

Explore opportunity

Use school choice and CTE strategically

School choice

CCSD offers zoned, open-enrollment, magnet, and specialized options. Program availability, capacity, transportation, and application rules differ, so families should compare the fit and confirm current deadlines on the official choice site (CCSD, n.d.-d).

Career and Technical Education

The CCSD CTE department supports more than 50 industry-standard programs of study and publishes career and course-planning resources. Cybersecurity is one available program area (CCSD Career and Technical Education, n.d.).

Request support

Start with the school and document the next step

Student services

CCSD Student Services provides special-education resources and links to specialized departments. For individual concerns, contact the school, ask which process applies, and submit requests in writing when a formal response is needed.

Resolve a concern

Describe the concern with examples, ask what policy or plan applies, identify the responsible person, and follow up in writing. Use district-level resources if the school cannot resolve the issue.

Family school check-in

Questions to ask together

  1. What does the portal show for attendance, missing work, and current grades?
  2. Which upcoming dates or decisions require action?
  3. Does the student understand how the grade is calculated?
  4. Are supports, accommodations, or services being implemented as written?
  5. What course, CTE, advanced-learning, or career option should we explore next?

Keep evidence

Build a simple school record.

Save the current schedule, report cards, transcript or credit summary, plans, evaluations, important emails, and notes from meetings. Record who agreed to each next step and the follow-up date.
