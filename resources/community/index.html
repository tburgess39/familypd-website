Local help by county

Find community support close to home.

Choose a county to find official resource directories, food assistance, health and housing support, school-family services, and organizations that can help families connect to local services.

Call or verify before visiting.

Hours, funding, eligibility, service areas, appointment rules, and available supplies can change quickly. Use the official website or phone number to confirm current information before traveling or sharing personal information.

Prepare before contacting an organization

Write down what support is needed.

  • What is the immediate need?
  • What county and ZIP code apply?
  • Is the situation urgent or ongoing?
  • What documents might the organization require?
  • What transportation, language, disability, or scheduling barriers should be mentioned?

Need help asking?

Use the Community Connector.

Prepare a respectful message, useful questions, and a follow-up plan before contacting a person or organization.
