Use the results as a conversation starter

Fair does not always mean mathematically equal. Health, disability, age, employment, school, caregiving, and safety can affect what each person can reasonably carry. The purpose is to make work visible and reduce avoidable overload—not to shame family members.

Children can help through safe, developmentally appropriate responsibilities while adults remain responsible for hazardous work and supervision. Families may also benefit from support beyond the household. The Community Connector can help identify relatives, neighbors, schools, faith communities, nonprofits, and other organizations that may provide information or practical support.
