Make household work visible.

Use the estimate as a conversation starter

Fair does not always mean mathematically equal. Health, disability, age, employment, school, caregiving, and safety can affect what each person can reasonably carry. The calculator is meant to reveal patterns—not assign blame.

Children can contribute through safe, developmentally appropriate work. Families may also consider relatives, neighbors, schools, faith communities, parent groups, nonprofits, and community services. Single parents and isolated caregivers may especially benefit from building support before a crisis.
