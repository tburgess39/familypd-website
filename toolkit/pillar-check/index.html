Five-Pillar Check-In

Reflect on what is going well and what may need support.

Rate concrete experiences instead of guessing what “struggling” means. The tool summarizes strengths, possible concerns, and a practical place to begin.

Educational reflection—not an assessment

This tool cannot diagnose health, relationship, financial, educational, or safety conditions. Seek qualified help for urgent concerns. Call 911 for immediate danger and call or text 988 in the United States for a suicide or mental-health crisis.

Your check-in summary

Complete any pillar you want to review. More answered questions produce a fuller picture.

How to use the results

Lower scores are not proof that a family has failed. They may show where stress, limited resources, health needs, conflict, or missing systems are affecting daily life. FamilyPD encourages families to start with health and safety, then choose one manageable next step.
