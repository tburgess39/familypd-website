Family Toolkit

Choose the tool that fits your family today.

Each tool works on its own. Families can begin with reflection, planning, communication, responsibilities, goals, or community connection.

Available tools

Start anywhere.

Select the tool that matches what your household wants to understand or build.

◎Reflection

Pattern Reflector & Support Ideas

Notice possible patterns involving basic needs, health, communication, workload, and community support. Receive matching ideas and links to other tools.

Helpful when:Your family senses that something is not working but needs clearer language for what may be happening.
Open reflector
◉Check-in

Research-Based Five-Pillar Check-In

Choose one pillar, answer a short set of practical questions, and watch research-informed praise, concerns, and next steps appear as you respond.

Helpful when:You want a focused check-in without completing a long whole-family questionnaire.
Open check-in
↔Workload

Household Workload Calculator

List visible and invisible work, assign ownership, and see whether one person is carrying most of the household load.

Helpful when:Work feels uneven, invisible, or dependent on one person.
Open calculator
☰Meeting

Family Meeting Planner

Plan updates, discussion topics, decisions, responsibilities, and follow-up.

Helpful when:The family needs a calm, organized place to talk.
Open planner
✦Direction

Mission & Vision Builder

Shape a family mission, vision, values, motto, and practical ways to live them.

Helpful when:The household wants shared direction and language.
Open builder
➜Goals

Family Goal Planner

Choose or customize a goal, break it into steps, identify support, and plan progress reviews.

Helpful when:The family knows what it wants to improve and needs a workable plan.
Open planner
◆Communication

Communication Style Reflection

Explore D, I, S, and C communication patterns and practical ways to communicate across styles.

Helpful when:Family members communicate, decide, or respond to stress differently.
Open reflection
⌘Community

Community Connector

Identify people or organizations that may help, prepare questions, and create a respectful outreach message.

Helpful when:Your family needs support, information, mentoring, advocacy, or stronger community connections.
Open connector
▤Discussion

Family News & Prevention Discussion

Use verified news stories to discuss prevention, safety, skills, and family systems.

Helpful when:A current event can become practical family learning.
Open collection
