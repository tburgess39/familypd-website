Why these questions are included

The official U.S. poverty measure compares family income with a threshold based on family size and composition. It does not capture every hardship a family may experience, so a household can face serious instability even without knowing whether it meets an official definition (U.S. Census Bureau, 2025).

CDC guidance connects children’s well-being with nutrition, water, movement, and sleep. The U.S. Surgeon General also identifies social connection as important to health and resilience, especially during difficult periods (Centers for Disease Control and Prevention [CDC], 2024; Office of the U.S. Surgeon General, 2023).
