Educational note

This reflector does not diagnose poverty, abuse, illness, family dysfunction, or crisis. It helps families name possible patterns and consider next steps. The official U.S. poverty measure uses income thresholds, but a family can experience serious material hardship even when it does not know its official status (U.S. Census Bureau, 2025). Health routines and social connection also affect well-being and resilience (Centers for Disease Control and Prevention [CDC], 2024; Office of the U.S. Surgeon General, 2023).
