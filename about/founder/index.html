About the Founder

Toni A. Hall

Educator · CTE Leader · Technologist · Curriculum & Program Developer · FamilyPD Founder

Toni Hall's work sits at the intersection of technology, education, workforce preparation, and practical systems. Her career has included software development, classroom teaching, district curriculum leadership, program management, cybersecurity and robotics instruction, and Career & Technical Education leadership. FamilyPD brings those experiences together around one goal: helping families gain practical knowledge, systems, and opportunities they can use in everyday life.

Professional journey

From programmer to educator to founder.

Each stage added a different lens: how systems work, how people learn, how programs operate, and how opportunities are built.

2006–2010

Programmer / Analyst

Developed, tested, documented, and improved web applications and databases while working with technical and managerial teams to solve business problems.

2012–2017

Instructor & Program Director

Built learning experiences in programming, multimedia, and web design while also managing a large credit-recovery program, staff, budget, data systems, and online-learning operations.

2017–2023

District Curriculum Coordinator

Supported Information Technology curriculum, certification integration, teacher coaching, technical support, district strategy, partnerships, and work-based learning.

2023–Present

Cybersecurity & Robotics CTE Educator

Delivers hands-on cybersecurity and robotics instruction with labs, simulations, project-based learning, employability skills, certification preparation, and student leadership opportunities.

2025–Present

CTE Department Chair
